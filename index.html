Internet Connectivity & Redundancy

Reliable connectivity is critical for node uptime and earning potential. Trujara has built multiple layers of protection:

  • Primary Connection: High-speed fiber optic link (300 Mbps+ symmetric) with low latency
  • Secondary Backup: Independent secondary ISP connection from a different provider with automatic failover
  • Tertiary Redundancy: 4G/5G cellular backup as final safety net
  • Power Protection: UPS systems protect both compute hardware and networking equipment
  • Monitoring: Continuous monitoring with automated alerts and seamless failover

This multi-layered approach ensures maximum uptime and protects against both internet outages and power-related failures.
