Decentralized AI vs Centralized AI

Centralized AI systems rely on a small number of large companies that control the data, models, and computing power. This creates serious privacy risks, potential censorship, and single points of failure.

Decentralized AI distributes compute and inference across many independent nodes. This approach significantly improves privacy because no single entity can access or control the entire system. It makes AI more resilient, private, and resistant to censorship.

By operating high-quality nodes, Trujara helps strengthen this decentralized model.
