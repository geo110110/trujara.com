DECENTRALIZED vs CENTRALIZED AI

Centralized AI systems concentrate data, models, and computing power in the hands of a few large organizations. This creates serious privacy risks, potential censorship, and single points of failure.

Decentralized AI distributes compute and inference across many independent nodes operated by participants worldwide. This model offers substantially better privacy because no single entity can access or control the entire system.

By running high-quality nodes, Trujara contributes to building a more private, resilient, and open AI ecosystem.
