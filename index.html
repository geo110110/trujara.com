Decentralized AI vs Centralized AI

Centralized AI systems rely on a small number of powerful companies that control the data, models, and compute. This creates risks around privacy, censorship, and single points of failure.

Decentralized AI distributes compute, inference, and control across many independent nodes. This approach offers significantly better privacy because data and processing are not concentrated in one location. No single entity can access or manipulate the entire system.

By running nodes, Trujara helps strengthen this decentralized model — making AI more private, resilient, and resistant to censorship.
