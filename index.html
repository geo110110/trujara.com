DECENTRALIZED vs CENTRALIZED AI

Centralized AI systems concentrate data, models, and computing power in the hands of a few large organizations. This creates serious privacy risks, potential censorship, and single points of failure.

Decentralized AI distributes compute and inference across many independent nodes operated by participants worldwide. This model offers substantially better privacy because no single entity can access or control the entire system.

DePIN and Privacy

DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks) allows computing resources to be provided by a global network of independent operators rather than centralized data centers. This significantly enhances privacy because data and processing are not concentrated in the hands of a few powerful entities.

Trujara supports the DePIN model by operating reliable nodes that strengthen the decentralized AI ecosystem and help protect user privacy.
